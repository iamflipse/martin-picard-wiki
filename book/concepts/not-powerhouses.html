Martin Picard: Mitochondria and Energy

Not Just Powerhouses

He keeps the ATP story and adds a signaling story.

The textbook line is that mitochondria are the powerhouse of the cell. Picard does not throw that out. On Mind & Matter with Nirosha Murugan he says they are also the place electrons flow from food to oxygen, and that this is not all they do.

"mitochondria are not just the place where electrons are flowing from food to oxygen and helping us create molecular uh you know ATP. They are also able to signal"

Mind & Matter episode 260, 29 October 2025. Caption.

On the Hyman show he pushes the picture further. The mitochondrion takes many inputs and behaves like a small brain inside the cell.

"The mitochondria behave like a little intracellular brain."

Mark Hyman, 19 November 2025. Caption.

That is the bridge to the mind. If the organelle that sets the cell's energy also signals, then stress, mood, and effort are not off to the side of metabolism. He calls the field mitochondrial psychobiology: the mind-mitochondria connection. The 2022 Mind & Matter episode spends its opening on that, including emotion and cognition depending on what mitochondria are doing inside neurons.

A later hunch, not a result: on Foundational Health he says the idea that mitochondria circulate and can be transplanted went from something people resisted to something companies are being built around. He then says:

"my hunch is in like 20 years, mitochondria transplantation will be probably frontline treatment in most hospitals."

Foundational Health, 6 November 2026. Caption.

He marks it as a hunch. The page leaves it there.
