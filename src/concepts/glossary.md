# Glossary

His usage in these interviews, not a textbook rewrite.

- **Mitochondria.** The organelles he refuses to leave as "the powerhouse." They move electrons from food to oxygen, make ATP, and signal. See [Not Just Powerhouses](not-powerhouses.md).
- **Mitochondrial psychobiology.** His name for the mind-mitochondria connection: stress, mood, and effort studied as energy biology.
- **Energy budget.** The roughly fixed energy the body can spend. Stress and extra demand take from other spends. See [The Energy Budget](energy-budget.md).
- **Energy resistance principle.** Flow needs some resistance, and there is a sweet spot. Too much resistance is the costly, hot state. See [Energy Resistance](energy-resistance.md).
- **Mitoception.** His word, on the Hyman show, for sensing your own energy state.
- **GDF15.** A protein in the blood he treats as a signal of energetic stress. High levels, in the biobank story he tells, track with more disease and earlier death. See [GDF15](gdf15.md).
- **Intrinsic health.** The measurable part of health under the skin. **Realized health** is what that becomes once tools and circumstances are included. See [Intrinsic Health](intrinsic-health.md).
- **Window, for gray hair.** His claim that color can return before a barrier, and that a full head of gray in old age does not. See [Gray Hair](gray-hair.md).
