# Gray Hair

**The finding he is asked about: graying can reverse, for a while, and not once the head is fully gray and old.**

Huberman opens his episode on the question people clip. Can less stress bring color back, and can more stress speed the gray? Picard says both are likely, and then the result:

> "I think what we discovered is that hair graying, at least temporarily, is reversible."

[Andrew Huberman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMkQUlBtFlk), 15 December 2025. [Caption](../interviews/2025-12-15-Improve-Energy-Longevity-by-Optimizing-Mitochondria-Dr-Martin-Picard.md).

On Diary of a CEO he says it again, and he says why it surprised him. He had learned that aging is a straight decline.

> "what we discovered is that hair graying is reversible."

The limit is in the same conversation. The mathematical model, as he tells it, says there is a window. A hair gets less efficient, energy resistance rises, then a barrier. Cross it, and the color change stays.

> "If you have a full head of gray hair and you're 70 years old, you're not going to regain your color."

He also answers the short-stress version. A hard two or three weeks that grays a bit of hair, then getting out of that period: he says the color can return. A whole head of gray in old age is a different case.

[Diary of a CEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xlmaorRY0w), 2 July 2026. [Caption](../interviews/2026-07-02-The-Mitochondria-Doctor-This-Reverses-Gray-Hair-Makes-You-Feel-Young-Again-Fixes.md).

Why the hair, in his account: the follicle is the last domino. The body has a finite budget and spends it by priority. Stress, the way he tells the presidents and the football managers, pulls energy toward whatever the body is treating as urgent. Pigment loses. The hair is a place you can see the reallocation. The Mind & Matter episode of 12 May 2022 is the long version of that study.

He does not give a recipe that turns gray hair dark. The mechanism he offers is the budget and the window, on [The Energy Budget](energy-budget.md) and [Energy Resistance](energy-resistance.md).
