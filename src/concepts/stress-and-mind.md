# Stress and the Mind

**Stress is an energy spend. The mind is not outside the mitochondria.**

The 16 percent figure on Diary of a CEO is his cost of worrying and ruminating: the stress hormone raised energy expenditure, and that energy has to come from somewhere that was keeping you well. The hair, the immune cleanup of damaged cells, and motivation are the spends he says lose. That accounting is on [The Energy Budget](energy-budget.md).

He uses a word for noticing the state from the inside. On the Hyman show he calls it mitoception: a sense of your own energy, practiced, not only measured. The caption has him stumbling onto the word ("I call this through mitoception") while describing a workshop exercise. The term is his. The sentence around it is messy because the caption is messy.

The mind-mitochondria claim in the 2022 Mind & Matter opening, from the host's setup of Picard's work, is that emotion, cognition, and behavior depend on what mitochondria are doing inside neurons. Picard spends that episode on stress, aging, and the gray-hair study as one visible trace of the same biology.

On Diary of a CEO he also says the high-resistance state shows up in mental illness: energy not flowing, lactate up, GDF15 up. He points at measurements of energy resistance in the brain from a group at McLean and Harvard. He is reporting that literature, not presenting a new brain scan of his own in that sentence. See [GDF15](gdf15.md).

What he offers instead of a drug name is allocation. Do not waste the budget on the past and the future. Spend it where the body can still adapt. The training half of that is [Move, Then Rest](move-then-rest.md). The limit on the visible marker is [Gray Hair](gray-hair.md): a window, not a promise that every gray hair returns.
