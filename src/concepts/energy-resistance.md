# Energy Resistance

**Flow needs some resistance. Too much resistance is the costly state.**

On the Hyman show he states the principle in one stretch. Existence as an energy process requires resistance. The place to live is a sweet spot. Not enough, and nothing is transformed. He reaches for a photon in empty space as the picture of no resistance and no work.

> "So the energy resistance principle says you your existence as an energetic process requires resistance but you need to live at a sweet spot of resistance."

[Mark Hyman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKpZ-ktgNRc), 19 November 2025. [Caption](../interviews/2025-11-19-How-to-Restore-Your-Body-s-Energy-and-Heal-from-Within.md).

On Mind & Matter with Murugan he uses the principle on a different puzzle. It is not the raw rate of energy flow that decides damage and the pace of aging. The point is how much resistance that flow meets. He says this resolves a conundrum in the numbers they were looking at.

The costly version shows up again in the gray-hair math on Diary of a CEO. A hair can accumulate damage, become energetically inefficient, sit in more and more energy resistance, and then hit a barrier. Before the barrier, color can return. After it, the change sticks. That model is what he says the math suggested. See [Gray Hair](gray-hair.md).

He also ties the principle to training. Resistance goes up while you work. The adaptation, including new mitochondria, is a low-resistance event. He says you make them when you are resting, not during the effort. That page is [Move, Then Rest](move-then-rest.md).

He does not present the principle as a finished clinical test. In these interviews it is the model he uses to hold the budget, the hair, the cytokines, and the training cycle in one frame.
