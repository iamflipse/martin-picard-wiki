# GDF15

**A blood protein he treats as a signal that something is running out of energy.**

On Diary of a CEO he walks through the path. Mental stress, with no exercise, raises a protein. He names it.

The protein reaches a region in the brainstem, the area postrema, which he also calls the vomiting and nausea center. The brain, in his telling, reads the signal as: something is running out of energy. Then two decisions, the same family as being sick. Save energy. Lose motivation. Feel depressed. Do not go to the gym. He says animal injections of GDF15 produce that hunched, inactive sickness behavior.

The second part of the account is the UK Biobank, as he describes it. Measure the protein, wait 14 years. People with high GDF15, compared with people with low GDF15, were more likely to develop mental illness, including bipolar illness, depression, and schizophrenia, more likely to develop cardiovascular disease and hypertension, and:

> "People with high GDF15 are more likely to be dead."

He says, at that point, they do not know whether the high reading came from mental stress, physical stress, or a sick organ. He also says people with high GDF15 are less likely to take the stairs, walk for pleasure, or go out with friends. The brain is getting a "running out of energy" signal, so effort feels like the wrong spend.

[Diary of a CEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xlmaorRY0w), 2 July 2026. [Caption](../interviews/2026-07-02-The-Mitochondria-Doctor-This-Reverses-Gray-Hair-Makes-You-Feel-Young-Again-Fixes.md).

He is asked how to prevent GDF15 from rising. He does not answer with a supplement stack in that stretch. He says there is initial evidence that it increases through life, and he folds it back into the budget: less wasted stress spend, and a life that does not leave the system in high resistance. On the Hyman show the same protein is the marker he wants for excess energy resistance. See [Energy Resistance](energy-resistance.md).

These are associations he reports from the biobank and from animal injections. He does not say that lowering one protein is a treatment.
