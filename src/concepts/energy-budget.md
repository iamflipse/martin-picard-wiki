# The Energy Budget

**There is a finite amount to spend. Stress spends it.**

The line he repeats is that the body has a fixed energy budget. On the Hyman show he says no one had a satisfactory account of it, and that the organism has to run an economy: measure expenditure over a day, and the books have to close.

> "there's a fixed energy budget that we have access to."

[Mark Hyman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKpZ-ktgNRc), 19 November 2025. [Caption](../interviews/2025-11-19-How-to-Restore-Your-Body-s-Energy-and-Heal-from-Within.md).

On Diary of a CEO he uses the same idea as the secret he is willing to name. Bartlett offers the sentence. Picard takes it up: if you do not waste energy on the future and the past, more is left for the moment.

> "the real secret to anti-aging, if there was one, is this. Is the proper allocation of energy."

He also describes an experiment from the lab. They wanted the cost of worrying about the future and ruminating about yesterday. The stress hormone raised energy expenditure.

> "the stress hormone increased energy expenditure [music] by 16%."

[Diary of a CEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xlmaorRY0w), 2 July 2026. [Caption](../interviews/2026-07-02-The-Mitochondria-Doctor-This-Reverses-Gray-Hair-Makes-You-Feel-Young-Again-Fixes.md).

With Shlloka he puts numbers on the same picture. A hundred units. Twenty of them go to worry, anxiety, feeling bad. Those twenty are not available for whatever else the cell was doing, including color in the hair.

Eating more is not the way through, in the Diary of a CEO telling. The budget does not grow because the plate does. Too much food, especially too much sugar, is extra voltage. The system overheats. He calls that overheating inflammation: cells burning too much and signaling other cells with cytokines.

On Modern Healthspan he uses the same budget for a cell that suddenly has a new demand, and for cancer that has spread. Metastases, he says, drain the body's fixed budget. That is his framing, not a trial result recited in that sentence.

The practical pair he keeps with the budget is on [Move, Then Rest](move-then-rest.md): you cannot add the expenditure of training and also keep every other spend, unless the machinery gets more efficient.
