# Intrinsic Health

**Health under the skin, which can be measured, and health as it shows up in a life.**

On the Mayer episode he says the word "health" was too loose to quantify. They split it. One part is intrinsic to the person, under the skin. It is biology, and it is also moved by psychology, social life, and the environment. The traffic goes both ways.

> "intrinsic health is that thing that's inside of us that can be quantified and then there's realized health"

Realized health is what that inside becomes in the world. His example in the same stretch is vision. If his eyes do not work well, that is a deficiency in intrinsic health. Glasses change what he can do. The realized part depends on the tool, not only on the eye.

[Emeran Mayer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jTuPVjXD-zU), 19 August 2025. [Caption](../interviews/2025-08-19-The-Mitochondrial-Key-to-Longevity-Stress-Holistic-Health-with-Martin-Picard-PhD.md).

On Foundational Health he says the concept was the start of a paper, and that a group of researchers spent two years on what health is. He does not, in that answer, recite a clinical score you can order on Monday.
