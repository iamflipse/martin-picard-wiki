# Move, Then Rest

**The effort raises resistance. The new mitochondria arrive when you stop.**

On the Hyman show he separates the two phases. You do not build the new mitochondria during the work. You build them after.

> "You make your new mitochondria when you're resting"

He calls that a key piece of the energy resistance principle. The ride, the run, the hard hour raises resistance. The downslope is when the cell changes. [Energy Resistance](energy-resistance.md).

[Mark Hyman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKpZ-ktgNRc), 19 November 2025. [Caption](../interviews/2025-11-19-How-to-Restore-Your-Body-s-Energy-and-Heal-from-Within.md).

On Diary of a CEO he uses the gym the same way, from the budget side. An hour telling the body what the future will demand. If the budget is capped, the only way to be ready next time is to get more efficient: more mitochondria, a more flexible system. The energy for that efficiency still has to come from somewhere. That is why he does not treat "add exercise" as free. Something else gives, unless the machinery gets better at the same spend. [The Energy Budget](energy-budget.md).

Sleep is the far end of the rest side. On Dr. Matt's Metabolic Kitchen he calls sleep the ultimate restorative state, the opposite of being afraid, worried, and anxious. That is his contrast, not a sleep prescription with hours attached.

Nothing on this page is a training plan. It is the cycle he describes: resistance up, then down, and the build happens on the way down.
