# About

This wiki is a guide to 24 long-form YouTube interviews with Martin Picard, professor of behavioral medicine at Columbia University and director of the mitochondrial psychobiology group. The earliest here is Bulletproof in February 2019. The latest are from November 2026. The list is [The 24 Interviews](interviews.md).

Each page reports what is said in those captions. It is not medical advice. Picard often marks an idea as a hunch, a model, or a mathematical suggestion. Those labels stay.

## How the words got here

The text is YouTube's own English auto-captions (`en-orig`). Each rolling cue kept its last line. Stutters and markers such as `[music]` are unchanged. Names slip. Huberman's close calls him "Martin Peard." "GDF15" is sometimes spoken in pieces. Quotes are copied from the caption files, not cleaned into textbook spelling.

Two uploads of the same episode were not saved twice: Dave Asprey's later copy of Bulletproof #565, and Mind & Matter's 2025 reupload of episode 70.

Left out on purpose: clips of these same interviews, recap channels, and lectures (TEDxCambridge, a UCSF grand rounds, the CCH speaker series, an academic seminar, a French public conference).
